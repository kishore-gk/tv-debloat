# Changes in this fork

This is a modified version of [Plume Launcher](https://github.com/aradhyas8/Plume_Launcher) by Aradhya,
based on upstream commit `4cb7762` (version 1.4). Modified by Kishore Kulkarni, October 2026. Like the
original, it is licensed under the GNU GPL v3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The git history records every change
file by file.

Built for, and tested on, a TCL 55" Smart TV Pro (Google TV, Android TV OS 14, 1080p UI on a 4K panel).

## Look and motion

- **Glass home screen in the style of tvOS.** Continue Watching fills the screen as a "Top Shelf": full-size
  artwork with the app, the show, the time left and a Resume button. Left/Right pages through it; page dots
  slide along when there are more shows than dots.
- **Higher-resolution artwork.** Watch Next pictures are requested at a larger size where the image service
  allows it (Hotstar and SonyLIV via Cloudinary, Prime Video, YouTube, Crunchyroll). Netflix only offers 853x480.
- **Frosted glass dock.** Logos only. It rests small (like the macOS Dock) and opens when focused, with a
  glass lens that glides between tiles and a light sweep on the focused one. The row glides to off-screen tiles.
- **Continue Watching size follows focus:** full size when it's selected, compact otherwise.
- **Big clock, date and weather** at the top right. Their colour comes from the picture behind them (its hue,
  with the brightness flipped, at least 4.5:1 contrast).
- **Glass All Apps drawer and settings sheet,** with continuous (squircle) corners throughout.
- **Every animation on one S-curve** (cubic-bezier 0.65, 0, 0.35, 1).
- **Performance on a GPU-bound TV:** the glass uses a pre-blurred copy of the picture instead of a live blur,
  animations change only transforms (never layout), and the slow artwork zoom pauses while the remote is used.

## New screens

- **News.** A newspaper-style screen (black page, serif type): about 40 country editions, built-in sections,
  your own topics, local news for your city. Hold OK on a section to move or hide it. It also opens after the
  home screen sits idle (30 seconds by default, adjustable or off) and works as the screensaver. A morning
  briefing card can lead Continue Watching from 5 to 11 am. Sources: Google News RSS, with descriptions
  and pictures filled in from Bing News and the publisher's page.
- **Calendar** (OK on the date). A month grid with holidays and festivals; the weeks scroll between months.
  Holidays come from Google's public holiday calendar for the TV's country (one small file every two weeks),
  with India's official 2026 and 2027 lists built in.
- **Weather** (OK on the weather line). Seven days from Open-Meteo: a week strip, the chosen day's conditions,
  an hourly temperature and rain chart, wind, UV, sunrise and sunset.

## Behaviour

- **Own interface sounds** (`res/raw`, generated by `tools/gen_sounds.py`): a short thud to move, a lower one at
  the end of a row, a warm tone for OK. Android's click is silenced; the TV's "Sound effects" switch still mutes them.
- **Home button:** a short press opens Plume and goes back to the start of the dock; a long press opens the
  notifications. After the TV starts, Plume covers Google TV's home without moving your focus.
- **Focus fixes:** focus no longer jumps to the dock while the apps are still loading after a cold start, and
  Left/Right on Continue Watching can't drop focus into the dock.
- **Self-update is off** (`FORK = true`), so the original's updates don't replace this build.
- **Smaller picture cache** (the current show and its two neighbours), for a TV with 2.4 GB of memory.
